# Five-year demo and portfolio chart — 4 November 2026

Implementation follows the [approved design](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-five-year-demo-design.md). Both Dashboard Portfolio Growth and Analytics default to 5Y and expose the stock-page range controls: 1D, 5D, 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y, 5Y. Numeric time axes retain proportional gaps and year-aware labels. Accessible selected buttons remain available during loading/errors/sparse states. The bounded history endpoint uses real range boundaries and final bucket samples; five calendar years include leap years.

The demo replays quarterly buys and partial sales over five years using actual source daily closes and as-of historical FX. AAPL and MSFT enter early; RELIANCE enters after the 2023 JFSL demerger so the account has no unmodeled spin-off entitlement. The 2024 bonus split changes quantity while preserving cost. Gross dividends are simulated as cash credits on ex-dates, with pre-ex-date entitlement and no withholding/reinvestment. Provider split-normalized closes/dividends are restored to contemporaneous units first. Historical samples are stale backfills; no intraday path is invented. Cutoffs with post-action units/cash but only pre-action closes are omitted until a new closing observation is available.

Schema 6 adds immutable account actions, dividend cash links and a durable demo manifest. Existing no-action accounts retain their behavior. Reconciliation includes action quantity effects, entitled quantities, dividend amounts/cash links and account sequence. The explicit owner-maintenance upgrade independently checks v2 trades/ledger/projections against the saved source artifact, backs up rows and source data, fences the worker, then atomically replaces recognized financial history. Credentials, sessions, user resources and cancelled orders remain. A repeat upgrade does not reset later activity.

Provider characterization: the five-year export spans November 2021 to November 2026, with approximately 1,250 equity and 1,300 FX daily observations per series. Reliance's 2024 pre-bonus prices and dividends restore correctly against the source's factor2 split.
